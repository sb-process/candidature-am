Steelworks quality axes

4 quality monitoring axes structured around a common cross-cutting constraint: the BOF metallic charge composition.

A3-1

Residual elements & segregation control

Issue

Cu and tramp elements cannot be removed in the BOF — direct link to decarbonization and product quality.

Monitored variables
  • Cu · As · Ni · Sn
  • Residual P
  • CC centerline segregation
  • Free B on press hardening grades
Levers

Scrap specifications, sequencing rules, P thresholds, speed / superheat / EMS settings.

EAF-DRI 2029 impact

Transitional phase (2029–2031): EAF fed with selected scrap — Cu ~0.05–0.10%, Cu constraint still active. DRI phase (2031+): DRI/HBI ramp-up (Cu < 0.02%) → Cu structurally relaxed → grade sheet threshold revision to be anticipated. New monitoring parameter: source_file_liquide (BOF/EAF).

A3-2

Inclusion control & dissolved H

Issue

Chemical cleanliness of liquid steel, casting incidents and surface defects.

Monitored variables
  • [Al]sol
  • Ca/Al
  • Nozzle clogging
  • Dissolved H
Levers

Al and Ca adjustment, slag basicity, FeO+MnO, RH degassing and cleanliness proxy monitoring.

EAF-DRI 2029 impact

DRI gangue: residual SiO₂ (2–5%) and Al₂O₃ (0.5–2%) modify LF slag → basicity drops if CaO not adjusted → degraded desulfurization → fine MnS. H₂: wet HBI (maritime site storage) → H 6–10 ppm at EAF exit vs 2–4 ppm BF-BOF → extended RH H degassing.

A3-3

C/N control — RH degassing

Issue

Same RH lever, but different consequences depending on the product segment.

Monitored variables
  • NGO: ultra-low C and N
  • IF: free N, Ti/N
  • Packaging: free N, Al/N
Levers

RH vacuum duration and level, Ti addition, heat-by-heat tracking, campaign-level monitoring.

EAF-DRI 2029 impact

N pick-up from EAF arc: N at EAF exit = 60–100 ppm (vs 20–35 ppm BF-BOF) → RH must denitrify 2 to 3x more → vacuum < 0.5 mbar, duration 25–35 min vs 15–20 min. If N > 80 ppm on IF grade: downgrading. C: C at EAF exit = 0.05–0.15% → longer RH decarburization → production cycle impact.

A3-4

Sequence thermal control

Issue

Thermal drift on long sequences, with effects on clogging or segregation.

Monitored variables
  • Ladle temperature
  • Duration between treatments and casting
  • Position in the sequence
Levers

Heat balance model, adapted superheat rule and MES predictive alert.

EAF-DRI 2029 impact

EAF thermal window: EAF exit ~1580–1620 °C (vs 1620–1660 °C BOF) → reduced thermal margin, increased clogging risk. Dual liquid feed: BOF + EAF cohabitation feeding the same CC → sequencing conflicts (low Cu/high N EAF vs high Cu/low N BOF) → 2–4 transition slabs to downgrade per feed changeover.

Main cross-cutting constraint — EAF-DRI 2029 integration

Decarbonization (higher scrap rate → EAF + DRI/HBI import by 2029) is the major strategic constraint. Its impact spans all 4 A3 axes:

  • A3-1 Residual elements: transitional high-Cu phase (scrap 2029-2031), then Cu relaxation via DRI — new parameter source_file_liquide (BOF vs EAF).
  • A3-2 Inclusion control: DRI gangue (SiO₂, Al₂O₃) modifies LF slag — high H from wet HBI requiring enhanced degassing.
  • A3-3 RH degassing: N pick-up from EAF arc 60–100 ppm (vs 25–40 BOF) — the RH must absorb 2 to 3x more N, with C also higher.
  • A3-4 Thermal: EAF exit ~1580–1620 °C (vs 1620–1660 °C BOF) — reduced margin, clogging risk — dual BOF/EAF liquid feed creating sequencing conflicts.

The A3 quality projects must integrate this transition by anticipating new constraints from within the current BOF framework.

Sub-Segment Sheets — NGO high Si & Packaging Tinplate

Specific problem, SSEG thresholds and A3 approach for the 2 sub-segments with demo data.

SSEG_NGO_highSi — M400-50A · Residual N + AlN → W/kg

NGO high Si (Si 2.90–3.30%) for EV motors — dominant cofactor: residual N + AlN precipitation

Problem: Free N > 5 ppm → AlN precipitation < 0.5 μm → iron loss W/kg degradation. Free N = total N − N(AlN) − N(TiN) — KNIME WF03 calculation. Never at end of long sequence (> 6 heats).

SSEG thresholds: C RH < 20 ppm · Free N < 5 ppm · Cu < 0.045% · Ca/Al 0.10–0.14 · W/kg 50Hz < spec

SSEG_PKG_Tinplate — Tinplate MR · Free N + strain aging

Tinplate / TFS low C: very thin products (< 0.2 mm) — dominant cofactor: free N not fixed by Al → strain aging

Problem: Free N not fixed by Al → mechanical strain aging. Cu + free N interaction: free N amplifies Cu embrittlement. Al/N ratio > 3.5 ensures stoichiometric fixation. Oscillation marks < 0.5 mm critical for thin strip.

Quality monitoring methodology — Streamlit app

Interactive application presenting the concrete methodology for quality monitoring. Three complementary views, illustrated on simulated data (1,820 coils, 2 years). Thresholds are dynamically calculated from the demo data.

1

Reference framework

What

Structure the territory: 4 SEG → 14 SSEG → 20 grades → 30 defects. Complete catalog per grade with critical variables, thresholds, dominant mechanisms.

Link to PILOTAGE_COMPLET

Each SSEG sheet mirrors the content of the 14 workbook tabs: defects, heat-by-heat KPIs, cofactors, dedicated KNIME workflows.

2

RCA Studies

What

Calibrate empirical thresholds on simulated data (long-term, 2 years). Two complete studies: DC06 / BLSR (949 coils, binary Y) and M400-50A / W_kg (871 coils, continuous Y).

Pipeline

Exploration → Variable selection → Modeling → Ishikawa → Reverse FMEA → Calibrated thresholds. Thresholds feed continuous monitoring.

3

Continuous monitoring

What

Operate daily, heat by heat. Operational dashboard with indicators per grade (5-6 indicators, OK/alert/action thresholds), readable by a shift supervisor in 30 seconds.

Content

Process Cpk vs EN standard · dynamic empirical thresholds (calculated from demos) · anticipated drifts with grade → SSEG → SEG drill-up · escalation scenarios.
